## Community announcement

**AION Sentinel 0.6.7 — Unfinished Beta**

Boss warnings + damage meter, with a simpler three-control window and automatic target/location recognition. Move action warnings and timer groups independently. Enable the meter only when wanted; it discovers its adapter automatically. If Npcap is absent, guided setup downloads its fixed official installer after your consent, verifies it, and opens the normal visible wizard. Cancelling or a required reboot keeps the meter off.

The catalog has 1,002 profiles, but only 223 have some warning support. The others identify encounters without supplying mechanics. Gameplay validation is pending; compilation and package integrity have been checked.
