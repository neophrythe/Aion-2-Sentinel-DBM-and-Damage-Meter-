# Published encounter references

Sentinel 0.6.10 — **Unfinished Beta**, by **Neophrythe & Dr.Tatsu**.

The Boss Library's **Encounter guide** automatically selects references for the selected catalog profile. It includes published reactions, approximate HP ranges and duration claims, and links each fact to its source. Difficulty-specific notes remain associated with the corresponding catalog mode. Reference-title associations do not add alternative screen names.

Publisher timing is kept by meaning: a cast duration differs from an effect lifetime, telegraph lead, hit delay, windup or hit spacing. An HP range differs from an observed phase transition. These reference values do not start HUD timers. Live cast-end estimates still require fresh measured remaining seconds or changing visible progress. Wave arrival at the player also depends on position and geometry.

The optional skill timing tooltip includes matching explicit-label research notes when the skill belongs to the selected NPC. Existing curated responses take precedence. The additional response path does not add skill triggers, HP thresholds or repeat intervals.

The compact asset contains 88 factual references, 48 typed timing fields and 42 profile associations. Identity-evidence counts are retained for 137 world-boss or high-GS profiles. Public combat logs often contain player hits and HP samples rather than boss cast or phase events. Those counts therefore describe source availability, not complete warning coverage.

Sources include [A2Naki's dungeon guides](https://a2naki.github.io/aion-2-draupnir-cave-guide.html) and [Aion 2 Maps](https://aion2maps.com/guides/draupnir/). Individual links and regional/build limitations accompany each reference. Full pages, player logs, research snapshots and unrelated bot material are excluded from the package.
