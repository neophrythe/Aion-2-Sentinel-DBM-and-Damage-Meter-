The public world list has 54 entries but 51 distinct profiles because three names occur in more than one region. Its 56 NPC templates are retained. High-GS scope includes 83 source instance-map variants whose explicit entrance requirement is at least 1,400. Six public Explore/Conquest variants explicitly supply qualifying GS. Internal Easy/Normal/Hard variants are not translated into those modes; no player's gear is measured.

The existing reference supplies 859 grouped exact English skill rules, 54 public HP hints and three guarded reactor hints. The retained Tetrata reinforcement reference at 50% HP requires its exact NPC template and one of eight proven maps. Added in 0.6.6, this reference contributes one supported profile to the whole inventory; it does not fill the 65 target-profile gaps.

Published guide material contains 35 encounter entries and 83 mechanic references. Twelve of 19 eligible published skill candidates have local ownership joins, yielding 23 response-rule associations across 17 profiles. These are structural joins, not successful live observations. Conflicting HP estimates, historical Ludra and Asian reference responses remain inactive in current Global warning rules.

Generated warnings require actual visible complete-line cues, confirmed twice. Static skill lists often mark text hidden; a database label cannot prove that the current client displays it. Damage-hit skill IDs are not a verified cast-start source. A measured cast-end estimate can follow an observed remaining-seconds display or a changing visible target-cast bar; it does not supply a complete boss timeline, individual wave impacts or recurring fight schedules.

When mode remains ambiguous, only identical enabled owned skill cues shared by every compatible variant can run. No variant HP hint or timer is activated from that fallback. Unknown map links and same-name normal/summon candidates remain unresolved when evidence is insufficient.

**0.6.8 is an Unfinished Beta; gameplay validation is pending.** Compilation, signed-file verification, archive CRC and extracted-file SHA matching were structural checks only. No new application or live-game behavior was executed for validation. Earlier versions' results are not reused as proof of this build.
